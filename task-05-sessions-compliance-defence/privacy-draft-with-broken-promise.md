# Workn Privacy & Data Protection Policy (DRAFT WITH BROKEN PROMISE)

## 1. Introduction
Workn is a coworking and shared workspace platform based in Lagos, Nigeria. We value your privacy and believe you should understand how your information is handled in plain language.

---

## 2. What Data We Collect
When you join Workn, we collect:
- Your name, email address, and phone number so you can access our workspaces and receive facility alerts.
- Your tax identification number (`taxId`) for Nigerian commercial invoice compliance.
- Session logs (IP address and browser type) to protect your account against unauthorized logins.
- Booking and payment records when you reserve desks or meeting rooms.

---

## 3. Account Deletion & Right to Erasure

### [THE BROKEN PROMISE - TO BE REMOVED]:
> *"If you decide to leave Workn, clicking 'Delete Account' guarantees that every piece of information about you, including your name, email, phone number, all past payment receipts, transaction logs, and server backup files will be instantly and completely destroyed across all our systems within 60 seconds with zero residual trace."*

---

## 4. How to Request Deletion
To request account deletion, members can submit an in-app erasure request via Account Settings or email `privacy@workn.ng`.
