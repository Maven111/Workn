# Terms and Conditions Legal Rationale

*Origin and legal review of the Workn Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.*

The Terms and Conditions used in Workn were adapted under the Laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria (incorporating commercial contracting standards and the Nigeria Data Protection Act).

---

## Section-by-Section Legal Commentary

### 1. Agreement to Terms & Scope
Solid opener. It correctly identifies the parties, establishes binding intent, and scopes the services (digital platform + physical coworking). "AI-supported operations" is scoped here and explicitly governed in Section 7.

### 2. User Eligibility & Account Security
Clean and practical. The 18-year age threshold is appropriate under Nigerian law. The non-transferability clause is essential for a coworking membership product to prevent unauthorized facility access.

### 3. Memberships, Subscriptions & Benefit Rules
Product-specific and tailored to Workn. The four callout blocks (Monthly, Non-Rollover, Day Pass, Expired Status) map directly to real business logic enforced in the system. The non-rollover clause is explicitly stated to prevent common member disputes over accrued credits.

### 4. Workspace & Meeting Room Reservations
The anti-overlap and capacity limit clauses reflect database-level constraints. The punctuality clause ("begin and end strictly at confirmed timestamps") establishes enforceable operational boundaries.

### 5. Pricing, Payments & Refund Policy
The 2-hour cancellation window for meeting rooms provides clear specificity over vague discretionary language. Payment verification occurs through server-side verification with the payment provider.

### 6. Physical Facility Conduct
Well-balanced across noise regulations, damage liability, and infrastructure caveats (power grid instability and secondary generator transitions). The force majeure clause accounts realistically for Lagos's urban infrastructure environment.

### 7. AI-Powered Member Assistant
Legally prudent and forward-looking. Clearly defines the boundaries of the AI assistant: fee waivers, membership tier adjustments, and refunds require explicit human administrative authorization and cannot be self-directed via AI tool calling. Protects the business against automated liability.

### 8. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution
Designates the High Court of Lagos State and the Federal Republic of Nigeria as governing jurisdiction. Establishes mandatory 30-day amicable negotiation prior to litigation, reducing legal overhead.
