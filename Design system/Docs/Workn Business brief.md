## **BUSINESS CONTEXT**

## **1\. The person who uses it**

Workn serves freelancers, remote workers, startup founders and small business owners in Lagos, Nigeria, typically aged 22–40. They need a reliable place to work, attend meetings, collaborate or get away from distractions at home.

Most users access Workn through a smartphone, while some use laptops for managing bookings, reviewing membership details and handling payments. They generally have basic digital literacy and are comfortable using apps, online banking and web services, but they should not need technical knowledge to use Workn.

Internet connectivity varies. Users may have reliable broadband in some locations but experience unstable mobile data, slow connections or occasional outages. The platform should therefore be mobile-friendly, lightweight, easy to navigate and clear about whether an action has succeeded, failed or is still processing.

## **2\. The one thing that must work**

Members must be able to verify their membership status and successfully book an available workspace without being given incorrect information.

Before confirming a booking, Workn must verify the member's active membership or day-pass eligibility, remaining benefits, resource availability and any applicable charges. It must then save the booking and return a confirmed status only after the system verifies that the operation succeeded.

This is essential because incorrect bookings, unexpected charges or false membership information can cost members time and money and damage trust in the business.

## **3\. Three questions the business gets asked repeatedly**

Question 1: Does Workn offer subscription model, and how can I pay for one?

Workn must retrieve its available membership plans, pricing, benefits and payment options, then guide the user through subscribing. It must verify the payment before activating the subscription and confirming that the member can access their benefits.

Question 2: How many coworking days or meeting-room hours do I have left?

The answer depends on the member's plan, benefit allowance and recorded usage. Workn must calculate the remaining benefits from the business's records, including any valid adjustments.

Question 3: Can I book a workspace or meeting room at my preferred time, and how much will it cost?

Workn must check real-time availability, booking eligibility, applicable membership benefits and additional charges before providing an answer. It should confirm the booking only after the booking record has been successfully created.

### **4\. Why the interface needs to feel a particular way**

Workn should feel professional, modern and welcoming, with the confidence of a reliable business service and the warmth of a shared workspace. Its users need to trust it with their money, memberships and bookings, while finding it easy to navigate during a busy workday. The interface should feel organised and purposeful without becoming cold, overly corporate or visually complicated.

This drives our typography choice: Space Grotesk for Display and larger Headline styles, paired with Manrope for the rest of the interface. Space Grotesk gives Workn a distinctive, contemporary character, helping the brand feel expressive and memorable. Manrope brings clarity and consistency to titles, body text, labels, forms, booking details and payment information. Together, they balance brand personality with practical readability, making Workn feel thoughtfully designed while keeping essential information easy to scan and understand.
