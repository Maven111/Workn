The whole ramp shifted about 20 tone steps toward white. The standard tones 10 to 30 run through deep, dark forest greens (`#002109`, `#003914`, `#005320`), while my pinned tone 10 to 30 are visibly brighter and more saturated (`#002C0E`, `#005120`, `#007933`).

At tone 40 the gap is largest, the standard palette gives a deep, muted green (`#006E2D`), while the pinned palette gives me a vivid brand green (`#16A34A`), which is almost the same colour as the standard tone 60 (`#1CA64D`). Everything above that follows the same pattern, so the pinned 60 to 80 are noticeably fresher and lighter than the standard ones.

From 95 upward the two palettes converge into near-white tints that are hard to tell apart.

Overall, the unpinned palette has more dark range and a more serious, forest-green feel. The pinned palette is brighter and friendlier, and its upper tones are slightly softer and more compressed.
